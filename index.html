Thought for the Week

"The degree to which a person can grow is directly proportional to the amount of truth he can accept about himself without running away."
— Leland Val Van De

Real strength is being able to sit with uncomfortable truths without making excuses or running away. Growth is not always about becoming someone new; sometimes it is about being honest with who you already are. This shows we are on the right path of self-discovery and self-acceptance, which will lead to personal transformation.
