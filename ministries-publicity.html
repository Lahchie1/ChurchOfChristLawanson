Publicity Ministry

The Publicity Ministry is dedicated to sharing the gospel through high-fidelity media, digital storytelling, and seamless live broadcasts.

Our Media Services

We leverage modern technology to ensure the message of Christ reaches every corner of the digital world with excellence.

Live Broadcast

Multi-camera HD streaming of every service to YouTube, Facebook, and our website, reaching hundreds globally.

Social Engagement

Connecting with our community 24/7 through curated spiritual content and updates across all platforms.

Digital Content

Short-form videos, sermon highlights, and digital devotionals produced weekly for online engagement.

Visual Identity

Crafting the visual narrative of Lawanson Church through professional branding, print media, and screen graphics.
