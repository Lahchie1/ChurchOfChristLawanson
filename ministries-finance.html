Financial Ministry

The Finance Ministry at Church of Christ Lawanson is dedicated to the transparent and faithful management of God's resources to advance His kingdom.

Our Financial Pillars

We operate under a strict framework of biblical stewardship, ensuring every widow's mite is used for its intended spiritual purpose.

Accountability

Monthly briefings provided to the church during its business meetings and quarterly reports for the entire congregation.

Strategic Expansion 2024

Our finance team works with ministry leads to budget for sustainable growth in youth education and community outreach.

Grace-Driven Giving

We believe giving is an act of worship, not a transaction. Every contribution fuels local and global missions.

Internal Controls

Multiple signatories and independent audit tracks protect every Naira donated. We adhere to international non-profit financial standards.
