Our History

For over five decades, Church of Christ, Lawanson has been a beacon of light, growing from a small gathering ito a vibrant community of believers dedicated to the Gospel.

Built on a Solid Foundation

Our mission is simple: to restore the New Testament church in its purity, simplicity, and doctrine. We envision a community where every soul finds grace, every heart finds a home, and every life is transformed by the Word of God.

Our Vision

To be a Christ-centered family impacting Lawanson and beyond.

Timeline

Following God's lead through the years

1970

The Humble Beginning

The first gathering of dedicated believers in a small living room, marking the birth of the Church of Christ, Lawanson.

1978

First Permanent Location

The congregation moved from house to house meetings into its first rented facility, signifying steady growth and commitment.

1985

Establishment of Leadership

The appointment of the first set of elders and deacons, bringing biblical structure and oversight to the growing congregation.

1998

Our Own Church Building

The congregation acquired and dedicated its own permanent worship hall at Ikate, Surulere — a dream fulfilled through sacrifice and faith.

2018

Ministry & Evangelism Expansion

Bro. Ogbu Otola began his work as the full-time preacher, leading to renewed focus on evangelism and spiritual growth.

2026

Divine Alignment

The church enters a new season with the theme “Divine Alignment,” trusting God for greater impact and spiritual maturity.

Be Part of Our Future Story

Our history is still being written, and there is a place for you in our community. Join us this Sunday as we continue this journey together.
