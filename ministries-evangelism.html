Evangelism Ministry

We are a community driven by the Great Commission, dedicated to sharing the transformative message of hope, love, and salvation from Lawanson to the ends of the earth.

A Vision Without Borders

Our evangelism efforts are focused on three core pillars of impact, ensuring no one is left behind in the dark.

Annual Bible Lectures & Indoor Evangelism

Every Saturday, our teams hit the streets of Lawanson and Surulere, bringing physical aid and spiritual nourishment to our direct neighbors.

Global Impact

Extending our reach beyond Nigeria through social media and supported mission points across the world.

Community Support

Holistic care programs including food drives, medical outreach, and literacy support.
