Who We Are

We are Christians only, striving to restore simple New Testament Christianity without denominational creeds. We teach the plan of salvation (hear, believe, repent, confess, be baptized for the remission of sins) and worship according to the New Testament pattern.

Our congregation is committed to following the Bible as our only authority in all matters of faith and practice. We seek to be nothing more and nothing less than the church described in the New Testament.

Non-Denominational

Wearing no other name but Christ, seeking unity through the Word rather than traditions.

Our Mission

To preach the gospel, edify the body of Christ and serve our community in Surulere and beyond.
