Worship & Services

Join us in fellowship as we praise God and study His word together. Experience spiritual growth and community.

Our Weekly Schedules

Regular opportunities for worship, Bible study, and collective prayer throughout the week.

Sunday Service

8:45 AM - 10:00 AM

Sunday School

Learn from indepth exploration of bible teachings, music classes, health talks and other teachings that need to be exposed.

10:15 AM - 12:00 PM

Morning Worship

Worship activities featuring the five (5) acts which are congregational singing, prayer, Lord's supper, giving and sermon.

6:00 PM - 7:00 PM

Evening Worship

A repeat of worship activities for brethren who were unable to attend morning worship and desire to worship.
