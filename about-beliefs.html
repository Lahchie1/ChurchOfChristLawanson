What We Believe

At Church of Christ, Lawanson, our faith is built on the foundation of the aposteles, with Christ being the Chief conerstone. We emphasize a return to the simplicity and purity of the first-century church.

Sola Scriptura

The Bible Alone

The Bible is our only authority in all matters of faith and practice. We reject human creeds and denominational traditions, holding firmly to the inspired Word of God.

Plan of Salvation

Hear, Believe, Repent, Confess & Baptism

We teach the biblical plan of salvation: Hear the Word, Believe in Jesus Christ, Repent of sins, Confess faith in Christ, and be Baptized for the remission of sins.

A Cappella Singing

Your Voice Alone

Our worship features vocal music only, without instrumental accompaniment, following the New Testament pattern of singing praises to God.

Lord's Supper

Upon the First Day of the week

We observe the Lord's Supper every Sunday, commemorating the death and resurrection of our Lord Jesus Christ.

Local Autonomy

Self-Governing

Each congregation is self-governing under the oversight of its own elders, with no central earthly headquarters other than Christ Himself.

Restoration Plea

Original Pattern of Worship

We seek to restore the simple Christianity of the New Testament, being Christians only and the church of Christ only.

Have Questions About Our Faith?

We would love to sit down and study the Bible with you. Whether you're curious about a specific topic or want to learn more about our community.
