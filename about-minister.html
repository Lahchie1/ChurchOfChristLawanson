Biograpahy

Evang. Ogbu Otola is the dedicated Minister of the Church of Christ, Lawanson. He faithfully preaches every Sunday, leads Bible classes, and oversees the spiritual growth of the congregation.

Through his leadership and teaching, Bro. Otola guides our congregation in understanding and applying God's Word to daily life. He is committed to preaching the gospel as it is, without compromise or alteration.

Bro. Otola leads our Wednesday General Bible Class and is actively involved in all aspects of our church's ministry and outreach efforts.

My Vision

To make the church a vibrant, Christ-centered community that reflects God's love through worship and service.

Community Impact

To engage with the Lawanson community through outreach programs and charitable initiatives.
